📱 Import OPML into Your Podcast App

Once your podcasts_rescue.opml is downloaded, follow these 2-step instructions for your client:

📻 AntennaPod (Android & F-Droid)
  1. Open AntennaPod → Tap Subscriptions.
  2. Tap + → Import / Export → OPML Import.
🔴 Pocket Casts (iOS, Android & Web)
  1. Open Pocket Casts → Tap Profile → Settings (gear).
  2. Tap Storage & Data Use → Import OPML.
🟠 Overcast (iOS)
  1. Open Overcast → Tap Settings (gear icon).
  2. Scroll to Nitpicky Details → Import OPML.
🍎 Apple Podcasts (macOS & iOS)
  1. On Mac: In top menu click File → Add Show by URL / Import.
