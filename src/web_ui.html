🎧 Podbean Export Walkthrough
  1. Log in to Podbean.com in your desktop browser.
  2. Open Developer Tools by pressing F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Opt+I).
  3. Switch to the Network tab in Developer Tools.
  4. Go to your profile page and click through your Followed Podcasts pages.
  5. Right-click anywhere in the Network tab and choose "Save all as HAR with content".
  6. Drop the saved .har file into the drop zone below!

📱 How to Import OPML into Your Podcast App

Once you download your .opml file, follow the quick instructions below for your favorite podcast player:

📻 AntennaPod (Android & F-Droid)
  1. Open AntennaPod and tap the Subscriptions tab.
  2. Tap the + (Add Podcast) button.
  3. Select Import / Export → OPML Import.
  4. Choose your downloaded podcasts_rescue.opml file.
🔴 Pocket Casts (iOS, Android & Web)
  1. Open Pocket Casts and go to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) in the top corner.
  3. Tap Storage & Data Use (or Import / Export).
  4. Tap Import OPML and choose your downloaded file.
🟠 Overcast (iOS)
  1. Open Overcast and tap the Settings (gear icon).
  2. Scroll down to the Nitpicky Details section.
  3. Tap Import OPML and select your podcasts_rescue.opml file.
🍎 Apple Podcasts (macOS & iOS)
  1. On macOS: Open Apple Podcasts, click File in the top menu bar → Add a Show by URL... or import OPML.
  2. Or air-drop / open the .opml file directly with Apple Podcasts.
🎧 Castro & Podcast Addict
  1. Castro: Settings → Sideload / OPML → Import.
  2. Podcast Addict: Tap the top + button → Tap OPML → Import OPML.
